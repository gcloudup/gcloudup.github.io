Fields & Field Types — Complete Beginner Guide

If you're new to Salesforce, fields are the very first building block you need to understand. Every object — Account, Contact, or a custom object you create — is really just a collection of fields, and each field decides what kind of data can live inside it.

What exactly is a field?

Think of an object as a spreadsheet and a field as a single column in that spreadsheet. Every record is a row, and every field is a column that holds one piece of information about that row — a name, a date, an amount, or a status.

Standard vs. custom fields

Salesforce ships with a set of standard fields on every object — things like CreatedDate, OwnerId, or Name. When those don't cover your business need, you create custom fields, which always end in __c.

  • Standard fields can't be deleted — only hidden or adjusted
  • Custom fields are fully editable — type, label, and properties
  • Custom fields give you full control over your org's data model

The five field type families

Once you're placing a new field, you'll be choosing from five broad families:

  1. Text — names, notes, free-form entry
  2. Number & Currency — amounts, percentages, math
  3. Date & Time — deadlines and timestamps
  4. Selection — picklists and checkboxes
  5. Relationship — Lookup and Master-Detail links between objects
A clean field naming convention today saves hours of confusion for the next admin who inherits your org.

A quick example

Here's what a simple Apex snippet looks like when it reads one of your custom fields:

Account acc = [SELECT Id, Loyalty_Tier__c FROM Account LIMIT 1];
System.debug(acc.Loyalty_Tier__c);
